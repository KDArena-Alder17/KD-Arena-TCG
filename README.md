# Kloak 'n' Daggurr's

*A game of deception and dominance, chaos, strategy, and endless conflict… where you forge your destiny in the shadows.*

In the world of **Kloak 'n' Daggurr’s**, 14 primal **Vigor Types** shape the realm: **Chaos, Earth, Fairy, Fungus, Greed, Lava, Lightning, Moon, Ocean, Purity, Sorcery, Spirit, Sun, and Tar**. Each Vigor flows through the lands, empowering decks and granting unique abilities to your cards.

Players construct their ultimate decks from five distinct card classes:

- **Vigor Cards** – Channel the raw energy of the Vigor Types to shape the battlefield.  
- **Primordial Beings** – Kings, Leaders, and Legendary entities that dominate the game.  
- **Creatures** – Fierce allies and monsters, each with their own unique powers.  
- **Runes** – Spells that twist fate, bend reality, and amplify strategies.  
- **Accoutrements** – Equipment and artifacts to strengthen your forces or sabotage opponents.

From the molten flows of **Lava**, to the silent tides of **Ocean**, the mysterious energies of **Moon**, and the ancient spirits of **Spirit**, each card type interacts with the vigor of the world, shaping endless battles and strategies. Only the most cunning and strategic players can harness these forces and emerge victorious.

Will you master **Purity**’s healing light, channel **Greed** to gain power at any cost, or embrace the chaotic unpredictability of **Chaos** itself? The choice is yours, and the shadows await.

---

## Game Overview

Kloak 'n' Daggurr's is a **turn-based trading card game** where players build decks of 60 cards, combining Vigor Cards, Creatures, Primordial Beings, Runes, and Accoutrements to dominate their opponents. Every deck is a personal strategy, blending the elemental forces of the 14 Vigor Types.

---

## Card Types

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Vigor** | Raw elemental energy cards from the 14 Vigor Types. |
| **Primordial Beings** | Legendary Kings, Leaders, and powerful creatures that shape battles. |
| **Creatures** | Allies and monsters, each with unique attack types. |
| **Runes** | Spells that manipulate the battlefield or disrupt opponents. |
| **Accoutrements** | Equipment and artifacts to enhance your cards. |

---

## Gameplay Basics

- Each player builds a deck of **60 cards**.  
- Battles are fought by summoning **Creatures** and **Primordial Beings**, casting **Runes**, and activating **Vigor**.  
- **Accoutrements** provide permanent bonuses or situational advantages.  
- The winner is the player who reduces their opponent’s life points to zero, achieves victory conditions, or dominates the field strategically.
